The Question

What is the vanishing gradient problem in deep learning?

Tap to reveal answer

The Definition

Vanishing Gradient

The vanishing gradient problem occurs during the training of deep neural networks when the gradients of the loss function approach zero, making it nearly impossible for the network to update its weights.

This typically happens in architectures with many layers (deep networks) when using activation functions like Sigmoid or Tanh, where the derivative is very small for large input values.

Key takeaway:

Results in very slow or stalled training of early layers in a network.
